# FitAlliance Pro — Feature pillars

## Core engine
- Runs member programs, check-ins, and progress in one coaching hub
- Coach dashboard rolls up who is on track without spreadsheet chasing
- Community feed keeps members engaged between sessions

## Workflow & output
- Trainers and studio owners stop stitching five apps together
- Progress logs surface in the coach view and the member view
- Program + community cadence from a single workspace

## Trust & control
- Decision-support for coaching ops — not medical or nutrition advice
- No fabricated member or retention counts
- Demo mode without a live API key
